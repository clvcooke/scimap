Federal science funding doesn't just support labs and universities — it fuels local economies, creates jobs, and drives innovation in communities across the country. Understanding how NIH funding is distributed at the congressional district level is essential for effective advocacy.

## Why Congressional Districts Matter

When it comes to science policy, members of Congress respond to the concerns of their constituents. By mapping NIH funding at the district level, we can show each representative exactly how much federal science investment flows into their community — and what's at stake if that funding is cut.

## The Data

Our analysis covers NIH funding data across all 435 congressional districts, including:

- **Direct grant funding** to research institutions
- **Indirect costs** that support research infrastructure
- **Employment impacts** including research positions and support staff
- **Economic multiplier effects** that ripple through local economies

## Key Takeaways

1. NIH funding reaches every single congressional district in the United States
2. The economic impact extends far beyond university campuses
3. Cuts to science funding have disproportionate effects on rural and underserved communities

We encourage advocates, researchers, and concerned citizens to explore our interactive maps to see exactly how federal science funding impacts their community. These data points are powerful tools for conversations with elected officials.
