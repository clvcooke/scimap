Our team tracks, analyzes, and visualizes the impact of federal science funding cuts across the United States. We analyze how policy decisions affect people and places by combining public data on federal grants, federal budget proposals, economic indicators, US census data on commuter flows, and community-level statistics.

Our research goes beyond numbers to tell the story of how science funding supports innovation, jobs, education, and economic growth in every state and territory — and what happens when funding gets cut.

Through interactive data visualizations and actionable insights, we make complex policy decisions understandable and their consequences visible to everyone, from concerned citizens to policy makers.

The team consists of experts from around the country, including researchers at the University of Maryland, the University of Pennsylvania, the University of Utah, the Georgia Institute of Technology, and the University of Oregon. The interdisciplinary team has expertise across a variety of areas including biology, psychology, neuroscience, communication, and geographic information science, united by a common goal.
