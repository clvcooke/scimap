**Angela Fagerlin, PhD** is Professor and Chair, Department of Population Health Services, Spencer Fox Eccles School of Medicine; Jon M. Huntsman Presidential Endowed Chair; and the Associate Vice President for Faculty at the University of Utah Health. She is internationally renowned for her research in risk communication and shared decision-making. Her research focuses on making medical information accessible to all patients and to helping guide patients to make medical decisions that best reflect their values and goals of care.
