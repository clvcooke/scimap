**Georgia Parolski, MPH**, is a Scientific Project Coordinator in the Department of Biology at the University of Maryland. She manages communications, outreach, and operations for SCIMaP. Her previous [work](https://www.wmar2news.com/homepage-showcase/whats-really-in-your-drinking-water-umd-study-aims-to-find-out) focused on coordinating a multi-county [drinking water study,](https://sph.umd.edu/marylandsafedrinkingwater) including environmental sampling, tailored public health communications initiatives, and large-scale surveys. Georgia has an MPH in Environmental Health Sciences from the University of Maryland and a BS in Biology from The George Washington University.
