**Lahne Mattas Curry** is a PhD candidate in Communication at the University of Maryland and the media and outreach specialist for the SCIMaP team. Lahne’s research focuses on trust, resilience, and information flow during environmental and public health emergencies, with particular attention to how institutional relationships, community engagement, and internal communication practices shape organizational capacity to function under pressure. Lahne has more than two decades of practical experience across government, corporate, and non-profit organizations. She most recently served as the Communication Director for the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency’s Center for Environmental Solutions & Emergency Response, which was eliminated in 2025.
