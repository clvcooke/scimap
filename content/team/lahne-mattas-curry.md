Lahne Mattas Curry is PhD candidate in Communication at the University of Maryland and the media and outreach specialist for the SCIMaP team. Lahne's research focuses on organizational relationships during environmental emergencies, with particular interest on community building, engagement, trust in institutions, access to accurate information, and organizational and internal crisis communication. Lahne has more than two decades of practical experience across government, corporate, and non-profit organizations. She most recently served as the Communication Director for the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's Center for Environmental Solutions & Emergency Research, which was eliminated in 2025.
