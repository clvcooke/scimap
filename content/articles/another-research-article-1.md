The Science & Community Impact Mapping Project (SCIMaP) has released its 2025 Annual Report, documenting a year of evidence-based analysis at the intersection of federal science policy and local economic impact. SCIMaP launched in March 2025 in response to an unprecedented wave of grant terminations, indirect cost cuts, and proposed budget reductions, SCIMaP translated public data on NIH and NSF funding in real-time, community-level maps that reached millions of Americans, informed Congressional advocacy, and supported national science policy organizations. The report details SCIMaPs research and engagement over the past year and lays out what comes next as federal science funding remains volatile and the stakes for local communities grow.
