---
title: SCIMaP team releases report analyzing FY 2026 White House budget
  proposals for NSF
order: 8
---
Last year, the SCIMaP team analyzed the projected impacts of the White House FY 2026 budget proposal, which aimed to cut NSF research funding by more than half, reducing NSF’s budget from $9 billion in FY 2025 to less than $4 billion in FY 2026. The White House’s proposed budget cuts would have lead to expected economic losses of ~$11 billion. NSF grants support the work of more than 100,000 research professionals and engage more than 200,000 undergraduate students, K-12 students, and K-12 teachers nationwide.
