---
The White House’s April 3 proposal would reduce NSF funding by about $6.4 billion (from $9.5 billion to $3.1 billion, inflation adjusted) and NIH funding by about $7 billion (from $36 billion to $29 billion, inflation adjusted). These reductions compare an inflation-adjusted 5-year baseline from FY2020-FY2024. The cuts diverge sharply from levels that Congress has set in the past through bipartisan support for federal science funding.
