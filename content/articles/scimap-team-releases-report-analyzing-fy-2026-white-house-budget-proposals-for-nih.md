---
title: "SCIMaP team releases report analyzing FY 2026 White House budget
  proposals for NIH "
order: 7
---
Last year, the SCIMaP team analyzed the projected impacts of the White House FY 2026 budget proposal, which aimed to cut NIH research funding by a reported $18 billion compared to FY 2024 levels. Budget cuts were projected to lead to more than $46B in lost economic activity in the upcoming year. Losses would be widespread, negatively impact local economies, reduce investment in areas of significant medical need, and lead to >200,000 lost jobs in addition to the NIH jobs that have already been terminated.
