title: Impoundment of Funds Endangers U.S. Investment in Science and Medical Research

---
In a brief Letter to the Editor in [Nature](https://www-nature-com.proxy-um.researchport.umd.edu/articles/d41586-025-03003-w) (September 2025), the SCIMaP team highlighted another threat to science: extensive delays in the distribution of funds that Congress appropriated for science and medical research. These delays carry enormous consequences. Research positions disappear, projects stall, and the broader innovation pipeline erodes. The Letter argues that Congress must exercise its oversight authority and ensure that agencies actually spend the funds it approves. The vast majority of the public supports continued investment in science and medical research. This message carries added weight in 2026, as both NSF and NIH have distributed research funds at a pace far below historical averages—and in some cases have distributed funds to projects that did not undergo peer review, raising new challenges for monitoring federal research investment in the year ahead.
