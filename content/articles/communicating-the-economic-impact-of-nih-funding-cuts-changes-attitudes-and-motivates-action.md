---
title: Communicating the Economic Impact of NIH Funding Cuts Changes Attitudes
  and Motivates Action
url: https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/bj857_v1
order: 5
---
In a SCIMaP preprint, Dr. Sinclair and colleagues tested whether information about federal funding cuts changes public attitudes and motivates action. The team ran two pre-registered online behavioral experiments with more than 5,000 participants using text, quizzes, and maps showing economic losses from NIH cuts, and also analyzed real-world sharing behavior and survey responses from over 22,000 SCIMaP website visitors. 

Across the political spectrum, participants who learned about the local and national economic impacts of federal science cuts reported decreased approval of those cuts, felt more informed, and expected greater negative effects in their local area. Interactive formats—personalized quizzes and dynamic maps—were most effective at motivating action, such as contacting congressional representatives or sharing information with others. These findings support the effectiveness of SCIMaP's approach for reaching broad audiences regardless of political affiliation.
