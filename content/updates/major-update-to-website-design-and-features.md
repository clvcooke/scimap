---
date: April 22, 2026
title: Major update to website design and features
---
We released an expanded and revamped version of scienceimpacts.org to improve the user experience and provide **new interactive maps** (check out the [gallery page](https://scienceimpacts.org/map)). We've expanded the scope of our analyses to cover **NSF impacts** in addition to NIH, and added new interactive features like zoominginto your **ZIP code** and **tables** that can be sorted and exported. Other new website features include a new **homepage**, an **Insights** section that compiles research reports and blog entries, and a **News** section with press releases and external news coverage. Code and data for this update are available on [Github](https://github.com/scienceimpacts/SciMap_Maps) and archived on [Zenodo.](10.5281/zenodo.19696708)
