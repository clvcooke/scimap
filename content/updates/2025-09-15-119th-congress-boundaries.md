---
date: September 15, 2025
title: 119th Congressional District boundaries
---

The Congressional District-level analysis has been updated to use boundaries from the 119th Congress (2025-2026) instead of the 118th Congress (2023-2024). This may lead to small changes in states that redistricted: North Carolina, Louisiana, Georgia, Alabama, and New York. We also now correct coordinates for a small number of organizations that we were previously unable to geolocate. This correction causes a small increase in estimated future losses.
